The rule of fives

Sea ice is pure ice with small inclusions of liquid brine. The brine volume fraction φ depends on temperature and bulk salinity; here salinity is fixed at 5 ppt and φ comes from the Frankenstein–Garner relation φ ≈ S·(49.185/|T| + 0.532)/1000 (T in °C, S in ppt). Columnar sea ice becomes permeable when φ reaches about 5% (Golden, Ackley & Lytle, Science 1998); for 5 ppt that is near −5 °C. The picture is a schematic: brine pockets are placed at random on a 2D lattice, with density chosen so the picture first connects at φ = 5%. Blue channels are brine connected to the ocean; the moving pulses suggest sea water flowing up through them.

海冰是纯冰里夹着很多小盐水泡。盐水占冰的比例 φ 取决于冰的温度和含盐量；这里含盐量固定为 5 ppt，φ 用 Frankenstein–Garner 经验公式计算。柱状海冰在 φ 达到约 5% 时开始透水（Golden、Ackley 和 Lytle，Science 1998），含盐量 5 ppt 时大约对应 −5 °C。图只是示意图：盐水泡随机放在二维网格上，密度经过调整，让这张图正好在 φ = 5% 时第一次连通。蓝色通道是和下方海水连在一起的盐水，里面移动的亮光表示海水顺着通道往上流。
