Six small interactive pages made while reading Golden et al., “Modeling Sea Ice” (Notices of the AMS, 2020). Each page has one slider. 1. Bond percolation on a square lattice. 2. The rule of fives: brine in sea ice and when water can flow through. 3. The 2D Ising model. 4. The melt pond Ising model of Ma, Sudakov, Strong & Golden (2019), next to a height-only version. 5. Ice–albedo feedback in a simple energy balance model. 6. Random fragmentation of ice floes. Every page runs offline in the browser.

这 6 个小程序是读 Golden 等人 2020 年的综述《Modeling Sea Ice》时做的，每页只有一个滑块，双击就能在浏览器里打开，不需要联网。1. 方格网边渗流。2. 五五法则：海冰里的盐水，以及冰什么时候开始透水。3. 二维 Ising 模型。4. Ma、Sudakov、Strong 和 Golden（2019）的融池 Ising 模型，旁边对照一个只看高度的版本。5. 简单能量平衡模型里的冰-反照率反馈。6. 浮冰的随机碎裂。
