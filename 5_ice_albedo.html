Ice–albedo feedback

Ice reflects sunlight; open water absorbs it. The planet’s temperature follows a simple energy balance.

A low-order energy balance model of Budyko–Sellers type: C dT/dt = Q·(1 − α(T)) − (A + B·T), with A = 202 W/m² and B = 1.9 W/m²/°C. The albedo α is 0.6 when T ≤ −10 °C (ice-covered), 0.3 when T ≥ 0 °C (ice-free), and linear in between. Q is the incoming sunlight. The temperature always moves from its current value toward an equilibrium, so the state depends on where the planet has been. The planet shows the ice cover implied by the albedo; the right panel traces every (Q, T) the planet has passed through, and marks where the temperature jumped. Golden et al. (2020) discuss low-order models of this kind. This toy model is global and very simplified; it illustrates the mechanism, not real numbers for the Arctic.

Budyko–Sellers 型的简单能量平衡气候模型：温度的变化 = 吸收的阳光 − 向外散的热，A = 202 W/m²，B = 1.9 W/m²/°C。冰多时反射率 α 高（0.6，T ≤ −10 °C），没有冰时低（0.3，T ≥ 0 °C），中间线性过渡。Q 是照到地球上的阳光强度。温度总是从当前的值出发，慢慢走向一个平衡温度，所以地球现在是什么状态，取决于它之前经历过什么。左边的星球显示对应的冰盖大小，右边的图记录星球走过的每一个 (Q, T)，并标出温度突然跳变的地方。Golden 等人（2020）讨论过这类简单模型。这个模型非常简化，只用来说明机制，数字不代表真实的北极。
