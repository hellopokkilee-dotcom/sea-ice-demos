The random fragmentation model from the floe size distribution section of Golden et al. (2020). The first floe always breaks. After that, every floe that is still active breaks into m = 4 equal pieces with probability p; with probability 1 − p it stops breaking for good. This is a branching process with mean offspring 4p, so it is critical at p = 1/4: for p ≤ 1/4 it dies out with probability 1, and for p > 1/4 it survives with positive probability. The expected number of floes at linear size 2−n grows like (4p)n, a power law with counting dimension D = ln(4p)/ln 2 (D = 2 at p = 1). Each run is one random outcome. Breaking stops at a minimum size of 1/256 of the side (8 generations). Blue floes are still breaking; white floes have stopped, either by chance or at the minimum size. Real floes also melt, refreeze, weld together and are broken by waves, which this model leaves out.

论文“浮冰大小分布”一节里的随机碎裂模型。第一块冰一定会碎。之后每一块还在碎的冰，以概率 p 碎成 4 块一样大的小冰，以 1 − p 的概率就此停下、不再碎。这是一个平均每代产生 4p 个后代的分支过程，临界点是 p = 1/4：p ≤ 1/4 时最终会停下，p > 1/4 时有一定概率一直碎下去。边长为 2−n 的浮冰的期望数量按 (4p)n 增长，是一个幂律，计数维数 D = ln(4p)/ln 2（p = 1 时 D = 2）。每跑一次只是一个随机结果。浮冰碎到边长的 1/256（8 代）就不再碎。蓝色是还在继续碎的冰，白色是已经停下的冰（随机停下，或者到了最小尺寸）。真实的浮冰还会融化、重新冻结、互相冻在一起、被海浪拍碎，这个模型都没有考虑。
