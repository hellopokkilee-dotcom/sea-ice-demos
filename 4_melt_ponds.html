The zero-temperature random-field Ising model of Ma, Sudakov, Strong & Golden (New J. Phys. 2019) on a 160×160 periodic lattice. Each site is water or ice and has a surface height hi, an independent standard Gaussian (light and dark shading). Both panels start from the same heights and the same random water/ice pattern. Left, the model: a site takes the majority state of its 4 neighbours; on a 2–2 tie, a low site becomes water and a high site becomes ice. Right, height only: each site simply follows its own height, so it always ends near 50% water. Every sweep visits all sites in a random order, and the run stops when a full sweep changes nothing: a metastable state. With J → ∞, each flip lowers H = Σi hisi − J Σ⟨i,j⟩ sisj; the paper also uses large finite J. The lattice constant a = 1 m, taken from snow-topography data, is the only measured input. The paper finds a pond size distribution with exponent ζ ≈ −1.58 (observed ≈ −3/2) and a transition of pond boundaries from simple to fractal near 90 m² (observed ≈ 100 m²). In the left panel, ponds larger than 100 m² are drawn in deeper blue.

Ma、Sudakov、Strong 和 Golden（New J. Phys. 2019）的零温度随机场 Ising 模型，网格 160×160，边界首尾相接。每个格子是水或冰，有一个随机的高度（图上深浅不同的明暗），格子之间互不相关。左右两张图用的是同一套高度、同一个初始水冰分布。左边是论文的模型：4 个邻居里水多就变水，冰多就变冰；2 比 2 打平时看自己的地势，低洼变水，凸起变冰。右边只看高度：每格只按自己的地势变，所以最后总是大约一半是水。每一轮按随机顺序把所有格子都检查一遍，直到整整一轮都没有格子变化才停下，这就是亚稳态。格子大小 a = 1 米，来自雪面地形数据，是模型里唯一一个测量得到的参数。论文得到的融池大小分布指数约为 −1.58（观测约 −3/2），融池形状大约在 90 平方米时从简单变复杂（观测约 100 平方米）。左图里面积超过 100 平方米的融池用更深的蓝色画出。
