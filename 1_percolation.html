Bond percolation

Each edge of the lattice is open with probability p.

Edge probability p

—

Bond percolation on a 30×30 square lattice: every edge is open with probability p, independently. The random numbers stay fixed while p changes, so the same random sample fills in as p increases. The cluster that joins the top row to the bottom row is shown in blue, and one shortest open path through it in orange. For bond percolation on the infinite square lattice the critical probability is pc = 1/2 (Harris 1960, Kesten 1980); on a finite lattice the chance of a top-to-bottom crossing near p = 1/2 is about 1/2.

方格网渗流：30×30 方格网上的每条边各自以概率 p 打开。拖动 p 时随机数不变，所以看到的是同一个随机样本随着 p 变大一点点长满。从最上面一行连到最下面一行的那一团通路显示为蓝色，其中一条最短的通路用橙色标出。无限大方格网上边渗流的临界概率是 pc = 1/2（Harris 1960，Kesten 1980）；有限大小的网格在 p = 1/2 附近连通的机会大约一半一半。
