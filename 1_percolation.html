Bond percolation

Each edge of the lattice is open with probability p.

Edge probability p
01/21
—

Bond percolation on a 30×30 square lattice: every edge is open with probability p, independently. The random numbers stay fixed while p changes, so the same random sample fills in as p increases. The cluster that joins the top row to the bottom row is shown in blue, and one shortest open path through it in orange. Dual lattice shows the dual graph: a vertex in the middle of every square, with a dual edge open exactly when the edge it crosses is closed. There is no top-to-bottom crossing exactly when a dual path joins the left side to the right side, and that blocking wall is highlighted. Estimate draws 1000 fresh samples at the current p and reports how many of them cross. For bond percolation on the infinite square lattice the critical probability is pc = 1/2 (Harris 1960, Kesten 1980); on a finite lattice the chance of a top-to-bottom crossing near p = 1/2 is about 1/2.

方格网渗流：30×30 方格网上的每条边各自以概率 p 打开。拖动 p 时随机数不变，所以看到的是同一个随机样本随着 p 变大一点点长满。从最上面一行连到最下面一行的那一团通路显示为蓝色，其中一条最短的通路用橙色标出。Dual lattice 显示对偶网格：在每个小方格中间放一个点，对偶边横穿原来的边，原来的边断开时对偶边才算通。从上到下连不通，当且仅当有一条对偶路径从左边连到右边，这道“墙”会被标出来。Estimate 在当前的 p 下重新抽 1000 个样本，统计其中有多少能从上连到下。无限大方格网上边渗流的临界概率是 pc = 1/2（Harris 1960，Kesten 1980）；有限大小的网格在 p = 1/2 附近连通的机会大约一半一半。
